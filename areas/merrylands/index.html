Merrylands to our workshop near Castle Hill

Merrylands is close: about 14 km from Stockland Merrylands to our workshop near Castle Hill, roughly 22 minutes in light traffic. Google Maps usually takes you through Parramatta and up Windsor Road. James Ruse Drive is a few kilometres longer but takes about the same time.

Merrylands station is on the train network, but you'd need to change to reach Castle Hill, so it's quicker to drive up from Merrylands Road.

MacBook Pro screens are different

14 inch and 16 inch MacBook Pros from 2021 onwards use a mini-LED display, sold by Apple as Liquid Retina XDR. These panels cost more than standard MacBook Air screens, so it's worth knowing the likely price before you come. Our pricing guide lists LCD only prices starting from $480 for a 14 inch M1 Pro or Max and from $580 for the 16 inch.

The lid matters even more on these models. If it's straight and dry, an LCD only replacement keeps your original lid and camera. If it's dented or has had liquid in it, a full display replacement is needed, and we price that at assessment.

Display features after a repair

Features like True Tone may not carry over after a screen replacement on some models. We explain what to expect for your model before you agree to the repair. The True Tone page has the background.

Quick check: call 0400 454 859 with your model number. Being so close, if the panel is in stock you can often drop off and collect the same day.

MacBook Screen Repair Questions

How far is your workshop from Merrylands?
About 14 km, near Castle Hill, roughly 22 minutes in light traffic via Windsor Road.
Do you repair MacBook Pro XDR screens?
Yes. We replace screens on 14 inch and 16 inch MacBook Pros, including the mini-LED models. Indicative prices are on our pricing guide, and we confirm the price at assessment.
Will features like True Tone still work?
On some models True Tone may not carry over after a screen replacement. We tell you for your specific model before any work starts.
Can I get it back the same day?
Often. Most LCD only repairs are same day when the panel is in stock. Full display replacements depend on parts.
