Mount Druitt to our workshop: M7 or toll free

From Mount Druitt station or Westfield Mount Druitt it's about 22 km to our workshop near Castle Hill. The quickest way is the M7, roughly 23 to 25 minutes in light traffic, but it's a toll road. Google Maps also offers a route via Bungarribee Road of about the same distance that takes around 30 minutes.

Mount Druitt station is on the Western line, but reaching Castle Hill by public transport involves at least one change, so most customers drive.

What happens when you drop it off

We start by checking the lid. We look for dents, a bent corner, gaps around the edge and any sign of liquid. Then we power the MacBook up to see how the panel is failing. That tells us whether an LCD only replacement is possible or whether you need a full display assembly. You get the price there and then, and there's no obligation.

When the panel for your model is in stock, most LCD only repairs are finished the same day. Full display replacements depend on parts and can take longer. We give you the timeframe before you decide.

Is it the screen, or something else?

Sometimes a "broken screen" isn't the screen. A completely black display with a working keyboard backlight, for example, can be a display fault or a logic board fault. A quick test with an external monitor (plug a TV or monitor into the USB-C or HDMI port) tells you a lot before you even leave Mount Druitt. If you get a picture on the monitor, the problem is in the display, which is what we fix.

Call before you come: 0400 454 859. Give us the model number from the underside of the MacBook and we'll check panel stock. Indicative prices are on the pricing guide; a 13 inch M1 MacBook Pro starts from $350 for LCD only.

MacBook Screen Repair Questions

How long does it take to drive from Mount Druitt?
Around 23 to 25 minutes via the M7, which is tolled, or about 30 minutes toll free via Bungarribee Road. It's roughly 22 km to our workshop near Castle Hill.
My screen is black but the MacBook seems to be on. Is it the screen?
Possibly. Try an external monitor first. If you get a picture on the monitor, the fault is in the display, which we repair. If not, it may be a logic board issue rather than the screen.
Do you offer pick up from Mount Druitt?
No. Repairs are drop off only at our workshop near Castle Hill, with no pick ups or mail in.
Is there a charge for the assessment?
The assessment is no obligation. We check the lid and display, explain the repair and give you the price before you decide.
