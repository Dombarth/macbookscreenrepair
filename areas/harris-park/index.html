From Harris Park, it's about 20 minutes

Harris Park sits right beside Parramatta, so it's one of the easier trips to our workshop near Castle Hill. It's about 14 to 16 km, roughly 22 minutes in light traffic. Google Maps offers James Ruse Drive, or Windsor Road and Old Northern Road, which is slightly shorter. From around the Little India shops and Harris Park station you're onto either one quickly.

If you're closer to the Parramatta CBD, see our Parramatta page.

One visit is often enough

Call 0400 454 859 first with the model number from the underside of your MacBook. We'll check whether a panel is on hand. When it is, and the lid is straight and dry, most LCD only repairs are finished the same day. You can drop it off, run your errands and come back.

If the lid has a bent corner, a dent along the edge or any sign of liquid, LCD only isn't possible and we'll quote a full display replacement. That can take longer, depending on when the part arrives.

About True Tone after a screen repair

On newer MacBooks, True Tone (the feature that adjusts the screen's colour to the room) can stop working after a screen replacement. Whether it carries over depends on the model and the repair type. We explain this for your model before we start, and the True Tone page covers why it happens.

Prices

Indicative LCD only prices by model are on the pricing guide. A 14 inch MacBook Pro M1 Pro or M1 Max, for example, starts from $480. Full display replacement costs more because the whole lid assembly is replaced.

MacBook Screen Repair Questions

Is there a MacBook screen repairer in Harris Park?
We're not in Harris Park itself. Our workshop is near Castle Hill, about 14 to 16 km away and roughly 22 minutes by car in light traffic.
Will True Tone still work after the repair?
It depends on the model and on whether it's an LCD only or full display repair. We tell you before any work starts.
Can I wait while it's done?
Most LCD only repairs are same day when the panel is in stock, but not usually while you wait. Drop it off and we'll call you when it's ready.
Do you take MacBooks by post?
No. Repairs are drop off only so we can check the lid with you before quoting.
