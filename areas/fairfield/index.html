Getting to us from Fairfield

Fairfield customers bring their MacBooks to our workshop near Castle Hill. It's about 21 km, roughly half an hour in light traffic, and most of the trip is one road: north along the Cumberland Highway (A28). From Fairfield Forum, Fairfield City Central or the shops around The Crescent and Ware Street you're on the highway within a few minutes. Allow longer at school pick-up times and in peak hour.

Fairfield station is on the train network, but getting from there to Castle Hill means changing services, so nearly everyone drives.

Before you head up the Cumberland Highway

A few things make the visit quicker:

  • Bring the charger. We power the MacBook up to see exactly how the panel is failing.
  • Note how the damage happened: a drop, something closed in the lid, or a spill. Liquid in particular changes which repair is possible.
  • Find the model number (it starts with A, on the underside), or call 0400 454 859 with it so we can check panel stock first.

We don't come to homes or businesses in Fairfield. Every repair is done at the workshop after an in-person check, because the condition of the lid decides the repair and the price.

Lines, flicker or a black screen

Not every screen fault is a crack. Coloured vertical lines, a flickering image, a dark patch or a screen that only works at certain lid angles all come in regularly. Many of these are the LCD panel itself, which an LCD only replacement fixes. Some point to the display cable or the lid, and then a full display assembly is the right fix. We test it in front of you and explain which one it is.

What it usually costs

Indicative LCD only prices are on our pricing guide. An Intel Retina MacBook Air (2018 to 2020) starts from $320 and a 13 inch M3 Air from $420. You get a confirmed price at assessment before anything is done.

MacBook Screen Repair Questions

Do you have a shop in Fairfield?
No. Our only workshop is near Castle Hill, about 21 km from Fairfield and roughly 30 minutes in light traffic via the Cumberland Highway.
Can you repair it at my home or business in Fairfield?
No. Repairs are drop off only. We need the MacBook on the bench to check the lid and test the panel properly.
My screen has lines but isn't cracked. Can it be fixed?
Usually. Lines and flicker are often a failing LCD panel, which LCD only replacement fixes. If the cause is the cable or lid, we'll tell you at assessment and quote a full display instead.
How long will I wait?
Most LCD only repairs are done the same day when the panel for your model is in stock. Full display replacements depend on parts availability.
