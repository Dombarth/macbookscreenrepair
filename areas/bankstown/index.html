From Bankstown to our workshop near Castle Hill

We don't have a shop in Bankstown. Our MacBook screen workshop is near Castle Hill in the Hills District, and Bankstown customers drive up to drop their MacBook off. From the Bankstown town centre it's about 26 km, which is roughly 35 minutes in light traffic via James Ruse Drive. The A6 is a similar distance. In peak hour, allow closer to an hour.

Public transport is harder than usual from Bankstown right now. The Bankstown Line has been largely closed since late 2024 while it's converted to Sydney Metro, so for most people driving is the realistic way to get to us.

Make the drive count

Because it's a fair trip each way, call 0400 454 859 before you leave. Tell us the model number printed on the underside of the MacBook (for example A2337) and what happened to the screen. We can then check whether a panel for your model is on hand. When the panel is in stock and the lid passes our LCD only checks, most LCD only repairs are finished the same day, so one trip is often enough.

LCD only or full display? LCD only swaps just the internal panel and keeps your original lid, camera and hinges. It needs a straight, undented lid with no sign of liquid. If the lid is bent or has been wet, a full display replacement is the only option. We check this at assessment and tell you the price before any work starts.

Studying or working in Bankstown?

If your MacBook is your study machine for Western Sydney University's Bankstown City campus or TAFE NSW Bankstown, mention any deadline when you call. We'll give you a real timeframe at assessment rather than a guess. If a full display has to be ordered in, you'll know before you decide.

A cracked screen almost never affects the files on the MacBook, but back up anyway if you can still see enough of the screen. If the display is completely black, a TV or monitor plugged into the USB-C or HDMI port will usually let you reach your files first.

Prices for common Bankstown repairs

Our pricing guide lists indicative LCD only prices by model. A 2020 M1 MacBook Air, for example, starts from $350, and a 13 inch M2 MacBook Air from $380. The final price is confirmed at assessment once we've checked the lid. See the full model list for your exact MacBook.

MacBook Screen Repair Questions

How far is your workshop from Bankstown?
About 26 km. We're near Castle Hill, roughly 35 minutes by car from Bankstown in light traffic via James Ruse Drive, and longer in peak hour.
Can I post my MacBook from Bankstown instead?
No. We only take MacBooks dropped off in person, with no shipping or mail in. We need to check the lid in front of you, because frame damage often doesn't show in photos.
Will I need to make the trip twice?
Often not. Most LCD only repairs are done the same day when we have the panel for your model. Full display replacements can take longer while parts arrive, and we tell you the timeframe at assessment.
Is there a charge just to look at it?
The assessment is no obligation. We check the screen and the lid, explain whether LCD only or full display applies, and give you the price before you decide.
