Greystanes is a short drive away

Greystanes is one of the closer suburbs on this side of Parramatta. From Greystanes Shopping Centre to our workshop near Castle Hill is about 16 km, roughly 23 to 25 minutes in light traffic. Google Maps suggests Old Northern Road or Seven Hills Road, both about 16 km. Old Windsor Road is a couple of kilometres longer but takes about the same time.

Greystanes has no train station of its own (the nearest are Merrylands, Parramatta and Blacktown, reached by bus), so it's easiest to drive.

Drop off in the morning, collect the same day

Being this close helps. If you call 0400 454 859 with your model number, we can confirm whether the panel is in stock. Most LCD only repairs are then done the same day. A morning drop off often means you can collect it later that day.

Why we need to see it: the price depends on whether your lid can keep its original frame. Dents, a bent corner or any sign of liquid rule out LCD only, and photos rarely show that clearly. The check takes a few minutes and there's no obligation.

Pressure marks and cracks from closing the lid

One of the most common kinds of damage is a crack or bright pressure mark from something left on the keyboard when the lid was closed, such as earbuds, a pen or a USB stick. The glass may look fine at first, then a line or dark patch spreads. That's damage to the LCD panel, and if the lid itself is straight it's usually an LCD only job.

Drops onto a corner are different. They can bend the aluminium lid, and then the whole display assembly needs replacing (see full display replacement).

Models and prices

We repair MacBook Air and MacBook Pro screens from older Intel models through to current M4 machines. See the model list and the pricing guide. A 13 inch M2 MacBook Pro, for example, starts from $380 for LCD only.

MacBook Screen Repair Questions

How long does it take to get to you from Greystanes?
About 23 to 25 minutes in light traffic. It's roughly 16 km to our workshop near Castle Hill, via Old Northern Road, Seven Hills Road or Old Windsor Road.
Can I drop it off and pick it up the same day?
Often, yes. Most LCD only repairs are done the same day when we have the panel for your model. Call first and we'll check stock.
Why can't you quote from a photo?
Because the lid decides the repair. A dent or liquid exposure means a full display rather than LCD only, and that's hard to see in photos. The in-person check takes a few minutes.
Do you replace the whole lid or just the screen?
Just the LCD panel when the lid is straight and dry, keeping your original lid, camera and hinges. If the lid is damaged we replace the full display assembly instead.
