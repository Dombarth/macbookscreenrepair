Guildford to Castle Hill

Our workshop is near Castle Hill, so Guildford customers drive up to us. From the shops around Guildford station it's about 20 km, roughly 26 minutes in light traffic via James Ruse Drive. Going through Parramatta and up Windsor Road is a little shorter (about 16 km) but usually takes about the same time. Woodville Road gets you onto either route quickly.

Guildford station is on the Old Main South line. Reaching Castle Hill by train means at least one change, so driving is simpler.

Is an older MacBook worth fixing?

Plenty of the MacBooks we see are a few years old: Intel MacBook Airs and 13 inch MacBook Pros that still do everything their owner needs. A new screen often makes sense on those because the indicative LCD only price is well below the cost of a replacement laptop. The pricing guide lists a 2015 to 2017 non-Retina MacBook Air from $250 and a 2016 to 2017 MacBook Pro 13 inch with function keys from $280.

If the battery is swollen, the keyboard is failing or the machine is very slow, a new screen might not be good value. We'll tell you if we think that's the case before you spend anything.

What to expect at the workshop

We power the MacBook up, look at how the panel is failing and check the lid for dents, bends and liquid marks. If the lid is sound, an LCD only replacement keeps your original lid, camera and hinges. If it isn't, we quote a full display replacement. Either way you get the price first and decide on the spot.

Save a wasted trip: call 0400 454 859 with the model number from the underside of the MacBook and we'll check panel stock for your model before you drive up.

MacBook Screen Repair Questions

How far is it from Guildford?
About 20 km to our workshop near Castle Hill, roughly 26 minutes in light traffic via James Ruse Drive. The route through Parramatta and Windsor Road is shorter and takes about the same time.
Do you repair older Intel MacBooks?
Yes. We replace screens on Intel MacBook Air and MacBook Pro models as well as Apple silicon machines. Indicative prices for older models start from $250 on our pricing guide.
Will you tell me if it's not worth repairing?
Yes. If something else on the MacBook makes a new screen poor value, we'll say so at assessment before you commit.
Do I need to book?
Call 0400 454 859 or use the booking page so we can check parts for your model. Repairs are drop off only.
