Lidcombe to our workshop

From Lidcombe station to our workshop near Castle Hill is about 20 km, roughly 25 minutes in light traffic via James Ruse Drive. The A6 is a little longer, at about 28 minutes.

Lidcombe station is a busy junction, but none of its lines reach Castle Hill without a change, so driving is the easy option.

If something was spilled on it

Liquid is the one thing that changes a screen repair the most. If coffee, water or anything else reached the keyboard or the lid, it can seep into the display area. Then an LCD only replacement isn't safe to do, and the right repair is a full display assembly. Tell us about any spill when you call. It's not a problem; it just helps us quote accurately.

If the MacBook got wet recently, turn it off and don't keep powering it on to test it. Bring it in as is.

A straightforward visit

Call 0400 454 859 with the model number from the underside of your MacBook. We'll check panel stock, then you drop it off. At the workshop we test the display, inspect the lid and tell you which repair applies and the price. Most LCD only repairs are done the same day when the part is in stock. Full display replacements can take longer.

Warranty: LCD panel replacements carry a 60 day limited warranty covering the new panel and our workmanship. It doesn't cover new physical or liquid damage after the repair. See our warranty page.

Indicative LCD only prices for each model are on the pricing guide. A 13 inch M4 MacBook Air, for example, starts from $450.

MacBook Screen Repair Questions

How long is the drive from Lidcombe?
About 25 minutes in light traffic. It's roughly 20 km via James Ruse Drive to our workshop near Castle Hill.
My MacBook had a drink spilled on it. Can you still fix the screen?
Usually, but liquid exposure rules out LCD only replacement, so the repair would be a full display assembly. Tell us when you call so the quote is accurate.
Is there a warranty on the repair?
LCD panel replacements come with a 60 day limited warranty on the panel and our workmanship. New physical or liquid damage isn't covered.
Can I send it by courier from Lidcombe?
No. We only take drop offs, with no shipping or mail in.
