Coming up from Homebush

Homebush is closer to the city than most of the areas we serve, but plenty of customers still come up to our workshop near Castle Hill. It's about 23 km from Homebush village, roughly 26 minutes in light traffic. The fastest route Google Maps suggests heads out via James Ruse Drive and includes a toll road. A Windsor Road route of about 24 km takes a similar time.

Homebush and Strathfield stations are close by, but getting to Castle Hill by public transport involves changes, so most people drive.

Is it worth the drive from Homebush?

That depends on what you need. Our focus is screens only, and the main thing we offer is a proper check of whether your MacBook can have an LCD only replacement. That swaps just the panel and keeps your original lid, and it costs less than replacing the whole display assembly. If your lid is straight and has never been wet, that check can save you a fair bit.

If the lid is damaged, the repair is a full display replacement wherever you take it. In that case convenience may matter more to you than the drive, and that's a fair call. We'll be straight with you either way.

Check before you come: call 0400 454 859, tell us your model number and describe the damage. We'll tell you whether LCD only is likely and whether the panel is in stock.

Common faults we see

Cracks from drops, pressure damage from something closed in the lid, lines across the image, a flickering backlight and screens that only show a picture at certain angles. Each points to a different part, so we test the MacBook before quoting. Most LCD only jobs are done the same day when the panel is in stock.

Indicative prices by model are on the pricing guide. A 15 inch M2 MacBook Air, for example, starts from $480 for LCD only.

MacBook Screen Repair Questions

How far is your workshop from Homebush?
About 23 km, near Castle Hill. Expect around 25 to 30 minutes in light traffic, and longer in peak hour.
Why come to you instead of somewhere closer?
We check whether your MacBook qualifies for LCD only replacement, which keeps your original lid and usually costs less than a full display. If your lid is damaged, a full display is needed anywhere, and we'll say so.
Do you have a drop off point in Homebush?
No. Our only location is the workshop near Castle Hill, and we don't accept MacBooks by post.
How long does the repair take?
Most LCD only repairs are same day when the panel is in stock. Full display replacements depend on parts availability, and we give you the timeframe at assessment.
