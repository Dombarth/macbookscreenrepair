It's a longer trip from Liverpool

Liverpool is one of the furthest suburbs we see customers from, so we'd rather be upfront about the drive. From Liverpool station to our workshop near Castle Hill is about 28 km, roughly 37 to 40 minutes in light traffic up the Cumberland Highway. Woodville Road is similar. Google Maps sometimes suggests the M7, which is longer in distance but about the same in time. In peak hour it can take an hour or more.

Coming from Westfield Liverpool or off the Hume Highway, the Cumberland Highway is the most direct way north.

Plan it so you only drive once

  • Call first: ring 0400 454 859 with the model number from the underside of your MacBook so we can confirm the panel is in stock.
  • Come in the morning: most LCD only repairs are done the same day when the part is here, and an earlier drop off gives the best chance of collecting it that afternoon.
  • Bring the charger: we need to power it up to test the display.

If the lid turns out to be bent or has had liquid in it, the job becomes a full display replacement. That may mean ordering a part and a second trip to collect. We'll tell you before anything is done, so you can decide whether that suits you.

Prices before the drive

You can get a fair idea of cost before you leave: the pricing guide lists indicative LCD only prices for every model we repair. A 14 inch M3 MacBook Pro starts from $550 and a 16 inch from $650. We confirm the exact price at assessment, and there's no obligation to go ahead.

MacBook Screen Repair Questions

How far is it from Liverpool to your workshop?
About 28 km. We're near Castle Hill, roughly 37 to 40 minutes from Liverpool in light traffic via the Cumberland Highway, and longer in peak hour.
How do I avoid two trips?
Call first so we can check parts for your model and drop off in the morning. Most LCD only repairs are finished the same day when the panel is in stock.
Can you collect from Liverpool or take it by post?
No. We don't do pick ups, shipping or mail in. MacBooks are dropped off at the workshop.
What if it needs a full display?
We'll tell you at assessment, with the price and the timeframe for the part, before any work starts. You can decide then.
